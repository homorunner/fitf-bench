# The Fox in the Forest 规则

## 概述 Overview

《林间狐》是一款专为两名玩家设计的吃墩卡牌游戏。玩家通过多轮出牌，争夺每一墩的胜利。每轮结束时，玩家根据赢得的总墩数获得分数，同时某些特定卡牌也会在出牌时带来额外分数。游戏结束时总分最高者获胜。

The Fox in the Forest is a trick-taking game for two players, each playing cards to win tricks against the other over multiple rounds. Players score points during a round by winning tricks containing specific cards and at the end of a round based on the number of total tricks they won. The player with the most points at the end of the game is the winner.

---

## 游戏设置 Setup

每局游戏由多轮组成。每轮中，一名玩家担任发牌员（首轮随机决定，之后轮流担任）。发牌员按以下步骤进行：

1. 洗混全部33张游戏卡牌。
2. 给每位玩家发13张手牌（各自查看，但须对对手保密）。
3. 将剩余7张牌面朝下放置，形成**抽牌堆**。
4. 翻开抽牌堆顶牌，面朝上放在抽牌堆旁。这张牌称为**法令牌**（其花色在本轮中至关重要）。

Each game consists of multiple rounds. In each round, one player will be the dealer (randomly selected for the first round, then alternating). The dealer completes the following steps:

1. Shuffle the 33 game cards.
2. Deal both players a hand of 13 cards (each may look at their own hand but keep it hidden).
3. Set aside the remaining 7 cards face down to make a **draw deck**.
4. Take the top card of the draw deck and place it face up next to the draw deck. This card is known as the **decree card** (its suit will be important during the round).

---

## 轮次概览 Round Overview

每轮包含13个**墩**。每个墩中，两位玩家各从手牌中打出一张牌：一名玩家**引牌**（打出第一张），另一名玩家**跟牌**（打出第二张）。根据两张牌的大小，决定谁**赢得**这一墩。

Each round consists of a series of 13 tricks. Every trick, both players play one card from their hand: one player will **lead** (play the first card), and the other will then **follow** (play the second card). Based on the two cards played, one player is said to **win the trick**.

---

## 出牌细节 Trick Details

**引牌**：每轮第一墩由非发牌员引牌。此后，除非特殊说明，上一墩的赢家成为下一墩的引牌者。引牌者可以自由选择手牌中的任意一张打出，该牌的花色称为本墩的**引牌花色**。

**跟牌**：跟牌者必须尽可能打出与引牌花色相同的牌（花色相同，点数不限）。若没有该花色的牌，则可以打出任意花色的牌。

**判定赢家**：两张牌打出后（且所有能力结算完毕），按以下规则判定：
- 若两张牌中有任意一张属于**王牌花色**（即法令牌的花色），则打出该花色中点数较大者赢得此墩。
- 若两张牌均不属于王牌花色，则打出引牌花色中点数较大者赢得此墩。

赢家将这两张牌收取，面朝下放在自己一侧。双方赢得的墩数公开可见，但不得翻看已赢牌堆中的牌面。赢家自动成为下一墩的引牌者（除非能力另有规定）。如此继续，直到13墩全部打完。

**Leading**: The non-dealer leads on the first trick of a round. After that (unless specified otherwise), the winner of one trick leads the following trick. The leader can choose any card from their hand without restriction. The suit of that card is the **lead suit** for the trick.

**Following**: After one player leads, the other must play a card matching the lead suit if possible (any rank). If the follower does not have a card in the lead suit, they may play any card without restriction.

**Determining the winner**: After both cards are played (and any abilities resolved), determine the winner:
- If either card is in the **trump suit** (the suit of the decree card), the player who played the higher-ranked trump card wins.
- If neither card is trump, the player who played the higher-ranked card in the lead suit wins.

The winner takes the two cards and keeps them face down near their side. The number of tricks won by each player is public information, but no one may look at the faces of previously won tricks. The winner leads the next trick (unless specified otherwise). Continue until all 13 tricks have been played.

## 卡牌能力 Abilities

所有奇数点数牌（1、3、5、7、9、11）均有特殊能力，在打出时触发；而偶数牌没有特殊能力。以下是具体能力表。

All cards of odd rank (1, 3, 5, 7, 9, and 11) have special abilities printed on them, which activate when the card is played.

| 卡牌 (Card) | 名称 (Name) | 能力 (Ability) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | 天鹅 (Swan) | 若你打出此牌并输掉了这一墩，你仍将**主导下一墩**的出牌。<br>If you play this and lose the trick, you lead the next trick. |
| **3** | 狐狸 (Fox) | 当你打出此牌时，可以**将法令牌与你手中的一张牌交换**（此能力可选择不使用）。新法令牌的花色将立即成为新的王牌花色，并用于判定当前墩的胜负。<br>When you play this card, you may exchange the decree card with a card from your hand. The new decree suit applies immediately, including to the current trick. |
| **5** | 樵夫 (Woodcutter) | 当你打出此牌时，**从抽牌堆抽1张牌**，然后**将1张手牌面朝下置于牌堆底部**。<br>When you play this card, draw 1 card. Then discard any 1 card to the bottom of the deck. |
| **7** | 宝藏 (Treasure) | **赢得此墩的玩家获得1分**（若一墩中有两张7，则赢家获得2分）。<br>The winner of the trick receives 1 point for each 7 in the trick. |
| **9** | 女巫 (Witch) | 在判定一墩的胜负时，若此墩中**只有一张9**，则将此9**视为王牌花色**来处理。<br>When determining the winner of a trick with only one 9, treat the 9 as if it were in the trump suit. |
| **11**| 君主 (Monarch) | 当你**引牌**打出此牌时，若对手有相同花色的牌，则其**必须打出该花色的1或该花色的最大牌**。<br>When you lead this, if your opponent has the same suit they must play either the 1 or highest card in that suit. |

关于部分能力的常见疑问，参见下文“附录：常见问题”。

For common questions about specific abilities, see the Appendix below.

## 轮末计分 End-of-Round Scoring

13墩全部结束后，统计每位玩家赢得的墩数。玩家根据赢得墩数获得分数（参见下表）。目标是赢得比对手更多的墩，但不可过度贪心。

| 赢得墩数 | 得分 | 描述 |
|---------|------|------|
| 0–3    | 6    | 谦卑 |
| 4      | 1    | 落败 |
| 5      | 2    | 落败 |
| 6      | 3    | 落败 |
| 7–9    | 6    | 胜利 |
| 10–13  | 0    | 贪婪 |

（若过度贪婪，将如童话中的反派一样自食其果。）

After all 13 tricks have been played, count how many tricks each player has won. Players score points based on the number of tricks won, as shown in the table below.

| Tricks won | Points | Description |
|------------|--------|-------------|
| 0–3        | 6      | Humble      |
| 4          | 1      | Defeated    |
| 5          | 2      | Defeated    |
| 6          | 3      | Defeated    |
| 7–9        | 6      | Victorious  |
| 10–13      | 0      | Greedy      |

(If you get too greedy, you will be brought down like the villain in so many fairy tales.)

---

## 游戏结束 End of Game

轮末计分后，若任一玩家累计达到至少35分，则游戏立即结束。否则，开始下一轮（由当前轮非发牌员担任下一轮发牌员）。继续进行完整轮次，直到有玩家达到35分或以上。最终总分最高者获胜；若总分平局，则比较最后一轮中双方仅按赢墩数获得的基础分（不含7号宝藏的奖励分），基础分较高者获胜。

After end-of-round scoring, if either player has at least 35 points, the game is over. Otherwise, play another round (with the dealer alternating). Continue until either player has at least 35 points. The player with the most points wins. If the total scores are tied, compare only the last round's base points from tricks won, excluding Treasure bonus points; the player with more base points wins.

## 附录：常见问题 Appendix: Common Questions

**Q: 3号（狐）和5号（樵夫）的能力何时触发？**  
A: 这两张牌的能力在打出后**立即**生效，在对手出牌或判定墩赢家之前。注意：若打出3号改变了法令牌，则王牌花色可能随之改变，新的王牌花色将用于判定当前墩的赢家。

**Q: 如果对手引牌打出一张9号（女巫），我必须跟9号的花色还是王牌花色？**  
A: 你必须尽可能跟9号的花色（即引牌花色）。非王牌的9号在两张牌都按正常规则打出之前，不会被视为王牌花色。

**Q: 如果对手引牌打出王牌花色的牌，而我手中有王牌花色的牌，我能打出非王牌的9号（女巫）吗？**  
A: 不能。非王牌的9号在两张牌都按正常规则打出之前，不会被视为王牌花色。如果你能跟出王牌花色，则必须跟王牌，不能打出非王牌9号。

**Q: 一墩中出现两张9号（女巫）时，谁赢？**  
A: 9号的特殊能力仅在一墩中**只有一张**9号时生效（两张女巫时魔法相互抵消）。若打出的是王牌9号，则王牌9号赢；否则，引牌花色的9号赢。

**Q: 一墩中出现两张1号（天鹅）时，谁引下一墩？**  
A: 若双方都打出1号，则由**输掉此墩**的玩家担任下一墩的引牌者。

---

**Q: When do the abilities on the 3 (Fox) and 5 (Woodcutter) occur?**  
A: Each of these abilities takes place immediately when the card is played, before another card is played or the winner is determined. (Note: If the 3 changes the decree card, the trump suit may change, and the new trump suit is used to determine the winner of the current trick.)

**Q: If my opponent leads a 9 (Witch), do I have to follow the 9's suit or the trump suit?**  
A: You must follow the 9's suit if you can. A non-trump 9 is not treated as if it were in the trump suit until after both cards have been played following the normal rules.

**Q: If my opponent leads a trump card and I have a trump card, can I play a non-trump 9 (Witch)?**  
A: No. A non-trump 9 is not treated as trump until after both cards have been played. If you can follow suit with a trump card, you cannot play a non-trump 9.

**Q: Who wins a trick with two 9 (Witch) cards in it?**  
A: The ability on the 9 applies only when the trick contains one 9. (With two witches, they neutralize each other.) If the 9 of trump was played, it wins; otherwise, the 9 of the lead suit wins.

**Q: Who leads the next trick after a trick with two 1 (Swan) cards in it?**  
A: If both players play a 1 (Swan), the player who loses the trick leads the next trick.
